Mouse: left drag = look around · right/middle drag = move · wheel = forward/back

Keys: L/R eye view · S toggle scene · O switch teapot/beam · T print matrices

VR controls Start: you stand inside the virtual head, facing the front wall
Left stick: walk · Right stick: turn left / right
Trigger (hold): grab teapot / beam, + right stick: distance / rotation
Holding with one trigger, press the other: spread / close hands to scale
Right stick click: switch teapot / beam
Left stick click: hide / show the help panel on your left hand
A/X: toggle scene · B/Y: reset
Grip: projection from your headset / from the fixed virtual head
